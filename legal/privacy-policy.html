Privacy Policy

Last updated: 1 July 2026

PolicyBhandar ("we", "our", "us") is committed to protecting your privacy and handling advisor, client, and content data responsibly. This policy explains what we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have, for both the PolicyBhandar Android and iOS apps.

Information We Collect

We collect the information needed to run your account and insurance-advisor workflows:

  • Account details: name, mobile number, email address, designation, date of birth, PIN code, LIC branch
  • Profile photo, if you choose to add one
  • Client/prospect records you enter into the app (name, phone number, and notes you add about your own clients)
  • Visiting card photos you scan, and text extracted from them for auto-fill
  • Content you download or share through the app
  • Subscription and payment status (processed by our payment partner — see "Payments" below)
  • Device details and app usage activity, for support and reliability purposes

How We Use Your Information

We use this information to provide app features, manage reminders, show relevant marketing content, process your requests, improve service quality, send important updates, and comply with legal obligations.

Payments

Subscription payments are processed by our payment partner, Razorpay. We do not store your card or bank details on our servers — Razorpay handles payment collection directly and shares only the payment status and identifiers needed to activate your subscription.

Camera & Photos

The app uses your device camera to scan visiting cards and, optionally, to set a profile photo. Scanned images are processed to extract text (name/mobile) to save you retyping it, and are stored as part of your account's records. You can decline camera/photo access at any time in your device settings, though the scanning feature will not work without it.

Information Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. Data may be shared with trusted service providers only when required to operate the app, process payments, deliver communications, or meet legal requirements.

Data Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational safeguards to protect your information. No online system can be guaranteed completely secure, so please also protect your own login credentials and device access.

Device Permissions

The app may request permissions such as camera, photo library, and notifications when those specific features are used. You can review and change these permissions at any time from your device's Settings app.

Your Data Rights

  • Access your personal data
  • Correct inaccurate data
  • Request data deletion, where applicable
  • Withdraw consent, where applicable
  • Ask how your data is used

Children's Privacy

PolicyBhandar is intended for licensed insurance advisors and business professionals, not for children. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy when our services, compliance needs, or product behavior change. Material updates will be reflected on this page with a new "Last updated" date, and may also be shared through the app.
